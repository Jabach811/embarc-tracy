Meet Daily Deals: A New Way to Save at embarc
August 31, 2026
Does anyone actually enjoy paying full price for stuff? We definitely don’t, so we launched
Daily Deals
. This brand-new program offers major savings on top-shelf
cannabis brands
across the menu, because your weed budget should go further.
Want to take advantage? As long as you’re a
Passport Club
member, you’re good to go.
Daily Deals are an exclusive perk for folks in the Passport Club. If you haven’t signed up yet, what are you waiting for?!
And these aren’t tiny discounts. Think 30% off
Heavy Hitters
carts, 40% off
Claybourne
pre-rolls, buy two, get one on
St. Ides
drinks, and SO MUCH MORE.
Passport Club members get exclusive access to Daily Deals like these.
Access to Daily Deals is just one reason to join Passport Club, too.
Members earn points with every embarc purchase, then redeem them for additional savings, sick merch, or unforgettable experiences like concert tickets and farm tours. Members also get access to Passport Day, an exclusive savings event that's basically the perfect excuse to stock up on all your favorite weed products.
Discover Your High with Daily Deals
embarc is all about helping you Discover Your High—and that’s just what the new
Daily Deals
are designed to do.
Each product category is color-coded, making it quick and easy to find a deal just for you. Big flower smoker? Look for the green column. Only into dabs? Check out the red section.
Here’s the cheat sheet:
Flower — Green
Carts — Yellow
Pre-rolls — Teal
Edibles — Pink
Extracts — Red
Wellness — Blue
No hunting, scrolling, or squinting required. Just find your color, find your deal, and get on with your day.
Ready to save big on your favorite weed brands? Sign up for Passport Club, head to your neighborhood
embarc location
, and lock in your Daily Deal. Your full-price weed era is officially over.
