WHAT IS EMBARC?
Click to expand answer
Embarc is a family of neighborhood shops celebrating cannabis for the curious, expert, and everyone in between. Our mission is to make learning about and shopping for cannabis approachable for all – no matter where you are on your cannabis journey.

WHAT PAYMENT METHODS DO YOU ACCEPT?
Click to expand answer
We accept cash, debit, and all major forms of electronic payment. There are also ATMs located in each of our stores.
+
IS THERE PARKING?
Click to expand answer
Yes!
All Embarc locations have free 20-minute parking adjacent to the shop.
Embarc Alameda has free 20-minute parking at the lot across the street.
+
HOW OLD DO I HAVE TO BE TO VISIT EMBARC?
Click to expand answer
You must be 21 years or older with a valid, government-issued photo ID. Guests under 21 must present a medical card along with their valid ID. Expired forms of ID will not be accepted.
+
I'M NOT SURE WHAT PRODUCTS ARE RIGHT FOR ME...
Click to expand answer
First off, don't worry – you've landed at the right place.
Embarc is all about guiding folks like you, no matter where you're at on your cannabis journey. Whether you're curious or are a seasoned consumer in the world of cannabis, we're here to help you find products that are right for you.
Whether you're seeking relaxation, a touch of euphoria, or just want to learn more, pop by one of our neighborhood shops to talk with one of our friendly, knowledgeable Guides.
We promise you'll leave with a smile, some knowledge, and the perfect product for your adventure.
+
HOW MUCH CANNABIS CAN I PURCHASE?
Click to expand answer
California law allows you to purchase up to one ounce of non-concentrated cannabis (flower) and eight grams of concentrated cannabis.
+
CAN I RETURN OR EXCHANGE PRODUCT?
Click to expand answer
Yes, with proof of purchase and original packaging.
+
DO YOU OFFER REFUNDS?
Click to expand answer
Yes, for defective product or product purchased by mistake. Proof of purchase and original packaging required.
