Shop Embarc California Cannabis Dispensaries

Embarc is one big family of neighborhood shops envisioned as a community where the curious, the expert, and everyone in between can discover and celebrate cannabis. No matter who you are or where you're from, Embarc makes cannabis easy (and fun!)

Every Embarc store delivers a memorable customer experience because every aspect of Embarc provides unparalleled service, quality, and value, no matter where you are on your cannabis journey. We put you first with our carefully curated menu. If it's here, you know it's good. We meet with every brand, review every product, and hand-select only the best for our shelves. Your dollar also flies further at Embarc because we get the best deals from our brand partners and pass the savings on to you.

But as much as we love to do all that for our people, quality and value mean more than just great weed at a great price. It's also about bringing a memorable experience to everyone who walks through our doors and giving back to the neighborhoods we serve. That's why Embarc donates 1% of sales to the communities we're in. That's right. When you shop Embarc, you support the place that you call home.

Get the Best Cannabis in California

Let's talk about the weed. After all, that's what we're all here for. Bottom line — Embarc is home to the best of California cannabis with a menu so deep we hit the sweet spot of every customer and every budget.

Our flower lineup includes iconic brands like STIIIZY, Claybourne, Traditional, American Weed Co, Yada Yada, and Pure Beauty, Zombi and LoLo lead the pack for pre-rolls with strains like Watermelon OG and Mr. Yum Yum.

If vapes are your thing, we carry selections from Almora Farms, West Coast Cure, Raw Garden, and Kurvana, and our selection of edibles is hard to beat with amazing brands like Wyld, Space Gem, Camino, Level Protabs, and Kanha. Embarc also carries a full lineup of concentrates, tinctures, and topicals from brands such as Papa & Barkley, Proof, High Power, and King's Garden, as well as a full lineup of CBD flower, CBD vapes, CBD edibles, and tinctures and topicals.
